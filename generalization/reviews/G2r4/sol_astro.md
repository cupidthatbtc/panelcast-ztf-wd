Overall: no BLOCKER in the D2 sinc algebra; the main risks are astrophysical transfer assumptions and D3 label attribution.

1. **MINOR — Boxcar algebra is correct.** [`d2_truth_model.py:58`](/Users/jackneo/Documents/vonhippel-base9/astro-wd/scripts/generalization/d2_truth_model.py:58) correctly uses \(\mathrm{sinc}(\pi T/P)\), divides out TESS attenuation, and reapplies ZTF’s 30-s attenuation. The 0.3 cuts are \(P=159.98\) s (120 s) and \(26.66\) s (20 s); all sidelobes peak below 0.3, so “reject shorter periods” is valid. Accepted catalog modes also have positive ZTF sinc. **Minimal fix:** call `cadence_s` an effective integration time and explicitly require midpoint timestamps; note that signed amplitudes are equivalent to \(|\mathrm{sinc}|\) plus a \(\pi\)-phase shift.

2. **MAJOR — Bandpass ladder is plausible, but not demonstrated to bracket DAV physics.** [`d2_truth_model.py:21`](/Users/jackneo/Documents/vonhippel-base9/astro-wd/scripts/generalization/d2_truth_model.py:21) The \(r/g=0.70\)–0.90 range is credible, and \(g/\mathrm{TESS}=1.4\)–2.1 plausibly covers many \(\ell=1\) DAV modes. However, the 1.43 blackbody calculation is neither band-integrated nor limb-darkening corrected; DAV ratios depend on \(T_{\rm eff}\), \(\log g\), and especially \(\ell\), with correlated—not independent—\(g/\mathrm{TESS}\) and \(r/g\). **Minimal fix:** label this a phenomenological sensitivity grid, and validate its endpoints using DA-atmosphere responses through the actual TESS/ZTF throughputs for \(\ell=1,2\).

3. **MAJOR — The \(\times\{0.7,1.3\}\) epoch axis is only a local sensitivity test.** [`GENERALIZATION_PLAN.md:203`](/Users/jackneo/Documents/vonhippel-base9/astro-wd/generalization/GENERALIZATION_PLAN.md:203) DAV mode amplitudes can change by factors exceeding 30%, disappear, emerge, or redistribute through beating and intrinsic variability. One common multiplier preserves an unrealistically fixed mode spectrum. **Minimal fix:** do not present ±30% as an astrophysical uncertainty envelope; add at least wider scaling and a mode-level dropout/independent-rescaling variant.

4. **MAJOR — Murphy flags provide class labels, not causal signal truth.** [`GENERALIZATION_PLAN.md:104`](/Users/jackneo/Documents/vonhippel-base9/astro-wd/generalization/GENERALIZATION_PLAN.md:104) `dSct=0` includes genuine γ Dor, rotational, binary, and other variability; hybrids may trigger through their low-frequency component. Calling its outcome “trigger rate” is correct, but detection-only completeness for `dSct=1` can likewise be credited to an unrelated component. **Minimal fix:** make frequency agreement with a cataloged p mode the primary attributed-detection endpoint; rename `dSct=0` the non-dSct comparison class.

5. **MAJOR — Mo super-Nyquist truth is aperture-level unless blends are excluded.** [`GENERALIZATION_PLAN.md:106`](/Users/jackneo/Documents/vonhippel-base9/astro-wd/generalization/GENERALIZATION_PLAN.md:106) A confirmed SN frequency establishes a sub-hour signal in the Kepler aperture, not automatically in the matched stellar source seen by ZTF. “Any SNF” also does not mean the per-star dominant amplitude describes that SN mode. **Minimal fix:** vet Kepler/Gaia contaminators, match the exact SN-mode frequency and amplitude, and verify whether Mo amplitudes already include Kepler long-cadence sinc correction.

6. **MAJOR — \(g=13.2\) is not a universal ZTF saturation boundary.** [`GENERALIZATION_PLAN.md:113`](/Users/jackneo/Documents/vonhippel-base9/astro-wd/generalization/GENERALIZATION_PLAN.md:113) KIC/Murphy \(g\) is only a proxy; saturation varies by band, detector, seeing, and epoch, while an A/F star satisfying \(g\ge13.2\) can still saturate in \(r\). **Minimal fix:** enforce saturation using ZTF band-specific photometry and epoch flags, and make the existing \(g>14\) safe subset the principal robustness result.
