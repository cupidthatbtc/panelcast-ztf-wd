# D3 negative-class trigger strata and covariates by class (descriptive, post-launch)

Post-launch descriptive analysis stratifies the unchanged 2,314-star rule-1 negative-class trigger numerator by fixed magnitude, Teff, merged-oid, pass, and sky cells and separately describes covariates by class; these are plain counts and rates without intervals, and the high-pass row is a negative-class trigger diagnostic rather than an FPR or sub-hour false-trigger estimate.

Admission: generalization/reviews/G5prep/sol_round2.md, item 6 (F15/F17/F27/F37, ADMIT-DESCRIPTIVE).
Frozen cuts: gmag <= 14.0; Teff (6597.0, 6737.0, 7092.5) K; RA (290.0945525, 293.54213, 296.340635) deg; Dec (41.048665, 43.879275, 46.70182) deg; merged oids <=1 / 2 / 3-4 / >=5 from the frozen crossmatch frame's selected_ztf_objects. Bins are left-closed/right-open; unknown cells
are explicit; zero cells are emitted; rates are blank at zero
denominators. The pass rows are each over all 2,314 negatives and are
not additive; the high-pass row is the "high-pass negative-class rule-1 trigger rate".
P3 itself is unchanged and none of these rows enters a headline,
endpoint, exclusion, reclassification, or replacement denominator.
